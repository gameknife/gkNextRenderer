The SDL3 Android Archive (.aar) is no longer placed here manually.

Gradle's `downloadSdlAar` task (defined in app/build.gradle) downloads
SDL3-devel-<ver>-android.zip from libsdl-org's official release and
extracts SDL3-<ver>.aar into this folder before each build.

To bump the SDL3 version, change `ext.sdlVersion` in app/build.gradle.
